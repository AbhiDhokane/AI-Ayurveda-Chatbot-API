About AyurvedaCare

AyurvedaCare is an educational platform that brings classical Ayurvedic wisdom into the modern world using AI. It combines a carefully curated encyclopedia of natural herbs and medicines with an intelligent chatbot powered by Google Gemini and Grok AI.

Our Philosophy

  • Prefer Natural: We prioritize herbal, Ayurvedic and lifestyle approaches over synthetic medicines whenever appropriate.
  • Rooted in Classics: Information draws from traditional concepts – Doshas, Agni, Ama, Rasa, Virya, Vipaka, Rasayana, Dinacharya and more.
  • Accessible: Multi-language support so knowledge can reach more people.
  • Responsible: Clear disclaimers and emphasis on consulting qualified practitioners.

Features

  • AI Chatbot that understands Ayurvedic concepts and prefers herbal recommendations
  • Detailed herb pages with classical properties and practical guidance
  • Search and category filters for easy exploration
  • Healthy living tips focused on natural fitness and balance

Technology

Built with Java + Spring Boot (backend & templating), HTML/CSS/JS frontend, and cloud-hosted AI APIs (Google Gemini & xAI Grok) for intelligent, real-time conversations. Herb data is stored in structured JSON for easy extension.

Disclaimer

AyurvedaCare provides traditional knowledge for educational and informational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. The information is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always seek the guidance of a qualified Ayurvedic doctor or healthcare professional with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or before starting any new herb or regimen.

How to Extend

You can add more herbs by editing src/main/resources/herbs.json. The chatbot system prompt and safety rules can be customized in AiChatService.java.
